TEMPER

The film follows a corrupt sub-inspector, Daya, who undergoes a moral transformation after a series of events involving a local don and a brutal rape case. Initially driven by greed,
Daya's redemption begins when he encounters Lakshmi, a woman seeking justice for her murdered sister.
Haunted by his past negligence, Daya resolves to bring the culprits to justice, even at the cost of his own life.
